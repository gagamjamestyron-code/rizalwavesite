A student’s point of view

My reflection

This is my reflection on the process of creating this website and what I learned about Dr. Jose Rizal.

About the creator

Gagam James Tyron B.

Student · Quezon City University

Before working on this website, I already knew that José Rizal was the national hero of the Philippines, but I only knew a few things about him. I did not really pay much attention to his life and contributions, so I mostly saw him as another person we studied in school.

One thing that stood out to me while working on this project was how Rizal fought for our country through his intelligence and writing. He used his works to show the problems happening during Spanish colonization. His writings helped Filipinos recognize those problems and inspired people to fight for freedom.

I would not say that this project completely changed how I see history. I still do not usually pay much attention to the past, but I understand better why Rizal is remembered and how much influence his ideas had on other people.

One lesson from Rizal that I think is still relevant today is speaking up about problems instead of simply ignoring them. I also think knowledge is more useful when we apply it, especially when it can help other people understand or deal with a problem.

Making this website was fairly easy and enjoyable. It gave me a chance to learn more about Rizal, organize information, and understand how the different parts of a website work together.

Gagam James Tyron B. · Student reflection
