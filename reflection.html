My reflection

This is my reflection on the process of creating this website and what I learned about Dr. Jose Rizal.

About the creator

Gagam James Tyron B.

Student · Quezon City University

At first, I saw this as another school requirement to finish, especially because I had other projects to work on too. While making this website, I started organizing what I knew about Rizal and what I still needed to check.

One thing that stood out to me is that Rizal was not only a writer; he also studied medicine and used his writing to discuss problems in the society around him. Building the website helped me organize the topic and present it in my own way.

My main takeaway is that history is more than memorizing names and dates. It also means looking at what people did in their time and why others still remember it.
